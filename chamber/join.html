Membership Level

Membership Levels

Non Profit Membership Level

NP Membership (Non Profit)

Cost: free

Benefits:

  • Access to community events at no cost.
  • Networking opportunities with other non-profit organizations.
  • Listing in the membership directory for local visibility.

This level is designed to support organizations that serve the community without financial burden.

Bronze Membership Level

Bronze Membership

Cost: $100/year

Benefits:

  • All NP benefits.
  • Access to business workshops and training sessions.
  • Discounts on chamber-sponsored events.
  • Company listing in the membership directory with a link to your website.

A great entry-level option for small businesses and startups to begin building professional connections.

Silver Membership Level

Silver Membership

Cost: $250/year

Benefits:

  • All Bronze benefits.
  • Promotion on chamber social media channels.
  • Sponsorship opportunities at local events.
  • Reduced rates for advertising in chamber publications.
  • Invitations to special networking events reserved for Silver and Gold members.

Ideal for growing companies that want to expand their visibility and influence.

Gold Membership Level

Gold Membership

Cost: $500/year

Benefits:

  • All Silver benefits.
  • Spotlight position on the chamber's home page.
  • Priority access and exclusive discounts for high-profile events.
  • Personalized business development consulting.
  • Premium advertising opportunities and features in chamber newsletters.

The premium package for estabilished businesses seeking maximum exposure and leadership within the community.
